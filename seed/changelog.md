# Changelog

Every Caelo release has its notes in two places:

- **In your admin:** Settings → Caelo updates (`/settings/updates`) shows the notes of every release newer than the one your install runs, before you update.
- **On GitHub:** [Releases](https://github.com/caelo-cms/caelo-cms/releases) and the full [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/caelo-cms/caelo-cms/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md), which follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

Caelo is pre-1.0. Until 1.0 it fails loudly with a structured error where it could otherwise fall back to a silent default, and a release can contain breaking changes; its notes say so. That rule is relaxed at 1.0 with a documented breaking-change policy.
